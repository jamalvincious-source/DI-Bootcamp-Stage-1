Uhuru Muigai Kenyatta

4th President of the Republic of Kenya (2013–2022)

Leadership & Legacy

Uhuru Kenyatta served as the fourth President of Kenya from 2013 to 2022. During his leadership, Kenya experienced significant infrastructural development, including the construction of the Standard Gauge Railway (SGR), modern highways like the Nairobi Expressway, and major expansions in electrical grid coverage and public healthcare access through the Big Four Agenda.

Timeline of Key Events

  • 1961: Born in Nairobi, Kenya, to Jomo Kenyatta (Kenya's first President) and Mama Ngina Kenyatta.
  • 1985: Graduated from Amherst College in the United States with a degree in Economics and Political Science.
  • 2001: Entered national politics upon nomination to Parliament and appointment as Minister for Local Government.
  • 2002: Ran as the KANU presidential candidate in his first bid for the presidency.
  • 2008: Appointed Deputy Prime Minister and Minister for Finance in the Grand Coalition government.
  • 2013: Elected as the 4th President of Kenya alongside Deputy President William Ruto.
  • 2017: Re-elected for a second presidential term focusing on the Big Four Agenda (Manufacturing, Affordable Housing, Healthcare, and Food Security).
  • 2022: Successfully completed his second term and transitioned power, subsequently serving as a regional peace envoy in the East African region.
